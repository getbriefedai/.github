# Briefed

**Briefed helps real estate agents stay consistent with the people they already know.**

Every Monday at 8:00 AM, Briefed texts a real estate agent the 3 to 5 people from their own
contacts worth reaching that week, the reason for each, and a ready message written in the
agent's own voice. The agent sends it. Briefed never contacts their clients.

Briefed is built by **Briefed Technologies Ltd**. We are pre-launch, starting with residential
agents in Tampa Bay, Florida (Hillsborough and Pinellas counties).

## The problem

Agents already know hundreds of people: past buyers and sellers, friends, referrals. Most of
those relationships fade because nothing prompts the agent to reach out, not because they
ended. A CRM stores the contacts. It does not say who to call this week, or why.

## How a week works

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Once, at the start | A 30-minute onboarding interview to learn how the agent writes, and an upload of their contact list (a CSV export from their CRM). |
| Every night | Briefed checks each contact against the agent's list and free county property records, looking for a reason to reach out. |
| Monday, 8:00 AM | A text with this week's 3 to 5 people, each with the reason and where it comes from. |
| The agent replies "2" | The ready message for person 2 arrives, to edit or send as it is. |
| The agent sends it | From their own phone, email, Facebook or CRM. Briefed never sends it. |
| Thursday and Friday | Short check-ins: who was reached, and whether it led to a conversation or an appointment. |

Agents use Briefed in three places, with no app to download and no dashboard to learn: text
messages (the main one), a Chrome side panel for copying messages next to Gmail, Facebook or a
web CRM, and a few short web pages for sign-up and settings.

## The reasons Briefed looks for

| Reason | When Briefed flags it | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home purchase anniversary | In the 14 days before the date | The agent's contacts or county records |
| Birthday | In the 7 days before | The agent's contacts |
| Long silence | After 180 days or more without contact | The agent's contacts |
| Ownership milestone | The month an owner reaches 5, 7 or 10+ years in the home | County records or the agent's contacts |
| Home value up | The county's value is up 25% or more since the last qualified sale | County records |
| Recorded sale | Soon after a new sale is recorded on a past client's home | County records |

The agent always sees the reason and where it comes from. County data comes from the free
public property roll and sales files published by the Florida Department of Revenue.

## Our commitments

- **The agent stays the sender.** Briefed only ever messages the agent. It never texts or
  emails the agent's contacts, and nothing is sent in the agent's name.
- **Only two data sources.** The agent's own contact list and free Florida public records. No
  bought data, no social media or LinkedIn scraping, no access to inboxes or calendars.
- **Consent first.** Consent is recorded before any contacts or writing samples are used, and
  data is deleted on request.
- **Texts on the agent's terms.** STOP and HELP always work, and Briefed sends no texts before
  8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM in the agent's time zone.

## How we will know it works

For each agent, one contact in five is set aside at random and never suggested. Briefed
compares what happens with the people it suggested against this control group: conversations
and appointments over 8 weeks, listings over 6 months, and recorded home sales over 12 months.
We do not claim results before the data supports them.
